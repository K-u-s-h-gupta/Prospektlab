Editorial Standards How pages are built
01 — Evidence first

Primary-source bias is deliberate.

Official filings, exchange disclosures, company material, and transcripts outrank generic summaries. The archive is designed to make that discipline easy to spot.

02 — Context where useful

Company context appears only when it adds real depth.

Ownership, governance, and filing context should sharpen the story, not decorate it. Thin coverage should stay clearly disclosed.

03 — Editorial finish

The output should feel publishable on first open.

Sharper typography, calmer hierarchy, and clearer routes make the archive feel closer to a financial publication than a content dump.
