Why research teams use it

Indian market research often starts in one tab and ends in five others. ProspektLab is designed to keep the core context together: filings, news, portfolio relevance, market data, and the working note you need to act on.

Filing context

Move faster from exchange disclosures to what changed, why it matters, and what needs verification.

Portfolio relevance

Connect company research to holdings, watchlists, and exposure instead of reading each update in isolation.

Repeatable notes

Turn research questions into structured notes that can be revisited, challenged, and improved over time.
