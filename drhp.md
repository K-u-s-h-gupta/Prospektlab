## **Automated IPO Analysis Playbook** 

# **(DRHP + ChatGPT)** 

## **Important Instruction (Read First):** 

**Use the prompts below after uploading the DRHP PDF of the Company to ChatGPT. ChatGPT will generate structured outputs. Final judgment must be taken by you using the interpretation rules provided.** 

## **System Instruction (use once at the start):** 

You are acting as a sell-side equity research analyst at a global investment bank. Your task is to analyze the uploaded DRHP strictly using disclosed information. Do not speculate or provide investment advice. Present all outputs in a professional, structured format. 

## **1. Business Quality Assessment** 

**Purpose:** Understand what the company does, how it makes money, and where it stands in the industry. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Summarize the company’s business model, key revenue segments, target customers, and industry positioning using the uploaded DRHP. Present this as an executive summary with key strengths and risks.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

+ Simple, focused business model 

- + Clear revenue drivers 

+ Dominant or defensible market position 

- **Negative Signal:** 

- Over-diversified or unclear revenue streams 

- Heavy dependence on single customer/segment 

- Highly cyclical or unproven industry 

## **2. Revenue & Profitability Trend Analysis** 

**Purpose:** Check whether growth is real and sustainable, not cosmetic. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Extract the last 3 years of revenue, EBITDA, and PAT from the DRHP. Calculate YoY growth and margins. Highlight trends.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

- + Revenue CAGR > **15%** 

- + EBITDA margin stable or improving 

- + PAT growth in line with revenue 

- **Neutral Signal:** 

Revenue growth **8–15%** 

Margins volatile but improving 

- **Negative Signal:** 

- Revenue growth < **8%** 

- Margins consistently declining 

- PAT growth significantly lower than revenue growth 

## **3. Growth vs Profitability Quality Check** 

**Purpose:** Detect growth at the cost of profitability. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Compare revenue growth with EBITDA and PAT growth over the last 3 years. Identify years where revenue grew but profitability declined.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

- + Profitability grows equal to or faster than revenue 

- **Warning Signal:** 

- Revenue ↑ but margins ↓ for **1 year** 

- **Negative Signal:** 

- Revenue ↑ but margins ↓ for **2+ consecutive years** 

## **4. Valuation vs Peers Analysis** 

**Purpose:** Check whether the IPO is fairly priced or aggressively priced. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Extract IPO price band, implied market cap, and valuation multiples from the DRHP. Compare these with listed peers mentioned in the DRHP.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

- + IPO valuation ≤ **peer median** 

- + Premium justified by higher growth/margins 

- **Neutral Signal:** 

IPO priced up to **20% premium** vs peers 

## • **Negative Signal:** 

- IPO priced **>30% premium** without superior fundamentals 

## **5. Promoter & OFS (Offer for Sale) Analysis** 

**Purpose:** Understand who is cashing out and why. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Analyze pre-IPO and post-IPO promoter shareholding and Offer for Sale details from the DRHP.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

- + OFS < **20%** of total issue 

- + Promoters retain > **50%** post-IPO 

- **Neutral Signal:** 

Partial exit by financial investors 

## • **Negative Signal:** 

- Large promoter exit 

- OFS > **50%** of issue size 

## **6. Use of Proceeds Quality Check** 

**Purpose:** Evaluate where IPO money is going. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Extract the ‘Objects of the Issue’ from the DRHP and categorize the use of proceeds.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

+ Capex, expansion, debt reduction 

- **Neutral Signal:** 

Working capital + general corporate purposes 

- **Negative Signal:** 

- Vague or unexplained fund usage 

- Majority for shareholder exit 

## **7. Risk Factor Severity Analysis** 

**Purpose:** Identify hidden downside risks. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Identify and categorize major risk factors from the DRHP and rank them by materiality.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Low Risk:** 

+ Generic industry risks 

- **Medium Risk:** 

Customer concentration, regulatory exposure 

## • **High Risk:** 

- Ongoing litigation 

- Regulatory dependency 

- High leverage or cash burn 

## **8. Institutional & Anchor Participation Signal** 

**Purpose:** Assess institutional confidence not prediction. 

## **Prompt:** 

“Summarize anchor investor participation and institutional allocation details disclosed in the DRHP.” 

## **Interpretation Rules:** 

- **Positive Signal:** 

- + Diversified anchor investors 

- + High institutional allocation 

- **Neutral Signal:** 

Limited but credible participation 

- **Negative Signal:** 

- Concentrated or minimal institutional interest 

## **9. Final IPO Signal Framework (Analyze Yourself)** 

After running all prompts, classify the IPO yourself: 

**Area Signal** Business Quality + / 0 / – Financial Strength + / 0 / – Valuation + / 0 / – Promoter Alignment + / 0 / – Risk Profile + / 0 / – 

## **Thumb Rule:** 

- **Mostly + → Strong IPO** 

- **Mixed → Wait & Watch** 

## • **Mostly – → Avoid / Post-Listing Review**
