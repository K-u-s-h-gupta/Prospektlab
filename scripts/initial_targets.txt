TCS Q4 FY25 results | Tata Consultancy Services is India's largest IT services firm. Q4 FY25 results cover revenue growth, operating margin, BFSI and retail vertical performance, FY26 demand outlook, attrition, and dividend declaration. Source: BSE and NSE filings.

ICICI Bank Q4 FY25 results | India's second-largest private bank. Q4 FY25 results cover NIM, deposit growth, loan book mix, GNPA/NNPA, credit cost, fee income, and FY26 growth commentary. Source: BSE/NSE filing.

State Bank of India Q4 FY25 results | India's largest public-sector bank. Q4 FY25 results cover NIM, deposit and advances growth, GNPA/NNPA and SMA pool, credit cost, treasury gains/losses, and dividend. Source: BSE/NSE filing.
Tata Motors Q4 FY25 results | India's largest CV maker and JLR parent. Q4 FY25 results cover JLR EBIT margin and free cash flow, India PV market share, CV cycle commentary, EV mix, and net auto debt trajectory. Source: BSE/NSE filing.
Larsen and Toubro Q4 FY25 results | India's largest engineering and construction firm. Q4 FY25 results cover infrastructure segment margin, hydrocarbons order inflow, services portfolio growth, IT services subsidiaries performance, and FY26 order pipeline. Source: BSE/NSE filing.
Adani Enterprises Q4 FY25 results | Adani group's incubator listco. Q4 FY25 results cover airports EBITDA, new-energy capex execution, primary industries margin profile, leverage, and group-level governance disclosures. Source: BSE/NSE filing.
Maruti Suzuki Q4 FY25 results | India's largest car maker. Q4 FY25 results cover domestic PV market share, SUV portfolio mix, export volumes, EBITDA margin, raw-material commentary, and FY26 capex guidance. Source: BSE/NSE filing.
RBI June 2025 monetary policy | The Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee meets in June 2025. Key items: repo rate decision, inflation projection for FY26, GDP growth projection, stance change commentary, liquidity management framework update, and any sectoral measures.
