FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this financial advice? expand_more

No. ProspektLab is a research tool that surfaces public data with citations. Every memo carries confidence tags and explicit conflict flags. You make the trade.

Where does the data come from? expand_more

NSE / BSE (NSE/BSE data + Yahoo Finance + Moneycontrol), Yahoo Finance v8 chart API, Wikipedia REST, Reddit JSON, Hacker News Algolia search, StockTwits public stream, and Google News RSS. We never invent figures.

Does it work for Indian stocks? expand_more

It works for any ticker that's publicly listed and has a Yahoo Finance entry. SEC coverage is US-listed only; for NSE / BSE-only names we rely on Yahoo, Wikipedia, news headlines and community signal.

What's the catch on the free tier? expand_more

There isn't one. We rate-limit if you go above ~50 runs/day per IP. Pro unlocks OpenAI-streamed prose synthesis, longer 4-round deep-dives, and watchlist alerts.

Can I export the memos? expand_more

Yes — every memo is downloadable as Markdown with the citation log preserved. Copy-paste into Notion, Obsidian, or your firm's research system.

Is my data private? expand_more

Everything runs server-side against public data feeds. We don't sell your watchlist or share queries. Self-hosting is also available for Team customers.
